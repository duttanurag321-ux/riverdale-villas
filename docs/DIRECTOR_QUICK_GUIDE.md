# Director quick guide (one page)

Six buttons at the bottom or side: **Home · Bookings · Money · Building · Messages · More**.

## Every day
1. Open **Home**. It lists only what needs you: payments to check, construction updates to approve, late payments, and customers it is a good time to ask for money.
2. Press the big button on each card. That is all.

## How a villa sale runs in the system
1. **Token** – when a customer pays a token: **Bookings → New booking**, choose the villa and customer, type the token amount. (Villa becomes *Reserved*.) Later check the token under **Money**.
2. **Booking** – open the booking, press **Confirm booking**. The 10% booking amount becomes due; the token already paid is subtracted automatically.
3. **Loan or own money** – on the booking page choose *Own money* or *Bank loan* (bank name, loan amount). When the bank sends money, your team records it with **Who paid? → The bank**.
4. **Before construction** – the system keeps construction *locked* until about 40% of the price is received (you can change the 40% in **More → Settings**). You can also press **Allow construction to start now** on the booking.
5. **When you need funds** – press **Ask for payment** (on the booking, or on Home). The screen shows a suggestion based on how much has been built. Pick the suggestion, 5%, 10%, everything left, or type your own amount (₹ or %). Press the button. The system then: tells the salesperson to call, and prepares a WhatsApp message with the construction update and the amount.
6. **Money comes in** – your team reports it, you check it in your bank under **Money → Verify payment**. A receipt is made.
7. **Foundation only / tin shed / full villa** – the same steps work. For now, choose your own amount if the suggestion does not fit (see "Limits").

## The suggestion – how to read it
"Construction is about 35% done. By now about 61% of the price should be received. You have received X." It is only a guide. You always decide.

## Never do
Don't share your password. Don't edit a payment: use **Reverse** if something is wrong (the original stays on record).

## Limits (honest)
- The suggestion counts all construction steps equally and assumes the full villa. For a foundation-only or tin-shed booking it will under-suggest; type your own amount. Per-scope step lists are a possible next step.
- WhatsApp messages are prepared by the system, but your salesperson taps Send themselves.
