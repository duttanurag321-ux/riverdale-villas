# Salesperson manual

You see Dashboard, Follow-ups, Customers, Bookings, Payments, Messages, Construction and Villas, limited to your own customers and bookings. You can never verify payments or change prices or plans.

1. **Today's tasks**: **Follow-ups**. Each card shows customer, villa, milestone, remaining amount and dates. A task appears automatically when a payment becomes payable.
2. **Call**: tap **Call**. **WhatsApp**: tap **WhatsApp** to open a chat (nothing is sent for you).
3. **Record the call**: pick the outcome (will pay on a date, wants more time, says already paid, no answer, wrong number, dispute, needs Director, other + note). For "will pay" choose the promised date; the same task moves to that date. Past promises are flagged for you.
4. **Report a payment**: **Report payment** (on the task or **Payments**). Enter booking, amount, date, method and the UPI/bank reference. It shows **Pending** and does **not** reduce the balance until the Director verifies it.
5. **Check verification**: **Payments** (your reports awaiting verification) or the booking page's ledger. Verified payments show a receipt number and PDF.
6. **Customer messages**: **Messages** shows ready-made WhatsApp texts. Tap **Send on WhatsApp**, press send in WhatsApp, then **Mark as sent** (or **Skip**).
7. **Open a customer's booking**: **Bookings**, **Open**: schedule, balances, ledger, **Statement (PDF)**.
8. **Overdue**: bell alerts and Follow-ups badges ("Follow-up overdue", "Payment overdue"). Repeated overdue reminders are prepared automatically and the Director is told after the configured days.
9. **Complete a follow-up**: it closes by itself when the milestone is paid; otherwise **Mark follow-up done**.
