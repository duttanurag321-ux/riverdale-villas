# Daily user manual

Pick your role: [Site Manager](SITE_MANAGER_MANUAL.md) · [Salesperson](SALESPERSON_MANUAL.md) · [Director](DIRECTOR_MANUAL.md).

**Everyone:** log in, check the bell for alerts, do your tasks, log out. If a button seems slow, wait: it shows a spinner and the screen dims; tapping again does nothing extra. Never share your password; ask the Director to deactivate a lost phone's user.
